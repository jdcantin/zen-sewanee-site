Sewanee Zen — Stone Nest Dojo 岩巣 — Sewanee, Tennessee

Stone Nest Dojo opened in April 2023 on 22 acres in Sewanee, Tennessee. It is the home and teaching base of Richard Reishin Collins, Abbot of the American Zen Association. We offer zazen sittings and retreats in the tradition of Master Taisen Deshimaru.
